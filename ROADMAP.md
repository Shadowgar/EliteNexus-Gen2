## Delivery approach

Work through one bounded requirement or coherent slice at a time, using the risk-based checks in [AGENTS.md](AGENTS.md). Phase 01D-W retains representative end-to-end gameplay coverage, but does not automatically repeat every unit test, toolchain or platform. Phase 02 uses measurements to identify actual bottlenecks and quality defects rather than speculative refactoring. Full cross-platform verification is reserved for an explicitly authorized acceptance need.

Phase 03 establishes a first-class OpenAI text assistant with bounded context, cancellation, validated tools and explicit authorization. Phase 04+ extends existing game-state and specialist capabilities into connected decisions, persistent goals, VoiceAttack interoperability, desktop command interfaces, paired tablet displays and VR. Evaluate compatible community integrations and their terms as needed; no repeated broad ecosystem audit or unauthorized gameplay automation.

No unfinished capability has a committed release date or version. Production service access, signing and publication require separate approval. Performance, accessibility, privacy, licensing and data integrity remain applicable throughout.

Use [BACKLOG.md](BACKLOG.md) for the requirement-level working queue. The [improvement register](docs/GEN2_IMPROVEMENTS.md) retains completed evidence and historical ENX identifiers. Earlier detailed research remains in its original repository; do not duplicate private materials into this public roadmap or load the entire audit for a routine assignment.
